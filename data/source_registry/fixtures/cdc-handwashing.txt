Community handwashing

Wash hands with plain soap and clean running water. Scrub the palms, backs of the hands, spaces between fingers, and underneath fingernails for about 20 seconds, then rinse and dry completely.

When soap and water are unavailable, an alcohol-based hand sanitizer containing at least 60 percent alcohol can be used. Sanitizer does not remove every type of germ or harmful chemical, so soap and water remain preferable in many situations.
