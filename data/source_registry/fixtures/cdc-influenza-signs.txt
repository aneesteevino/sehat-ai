Influenza signs and course

Uncomplicated influenza often begins suddenly and can include fever, chills, muscle aches, headache, tiredness, dry cough, sore throat, and a runny nose. Fever can be absent, particularly in some older adults or people with weakened immune systems.

For many people, uncomplicated symptoms improve within three to seven days, although cough and tiredness can continue for longer. Symptoms alone do not establish a diagnosis.
